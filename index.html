Hello, I'm

Xinyuan Yu

Ph.D. Candidate·MIT Earth, Atmospheric and Planetary Sciences

I am interested in how short-lived climate forcers affect air quality, climate, and human health. My current work focuses on tropospheric ozone, an air pollutant near the surface and a potent greenhouse gas, especially in the upper troposphere.

At MIT, I work with Prof. Arlene Fiore in the Fiore Group, combining chemistry–climate modeling with satellite, aircraft, ozonesonde, and surface observations to understand what drives tropospheric ozone trends and how they affect climate. Before MIT, I earned my B.A. and M.S. in Environmental Sciences at Peking University with Prof. Shu Tao. My work examined the health benefits of cleaner household energy and air-quality policies, and distinguished the effects of emissions and meteorology on black carbon.
